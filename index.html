Margin vs. Markup — Why it matters

Margin = profit ÷ selling price. Markup = profit ÷ cost. A 40% margin requires dividing cost by (1 − 0.40):

  • Total job cost $150.00
  • Required price for 40% margin ($150 ÷ 0.60) $250.00
  • Gross profit $100.00 (40.0%)

Important Notes

• Your time counts: Include the owner's labour cost per hour even if you don't pay yourself a formal wage.

• Sales tax: Enter selling prices excluding separately charged sales tax. Tax-inclusive totals are informational and not counted as profit.

• Planning tool: Results depend on your real costs and are not accounting, tax, or legal advice.
